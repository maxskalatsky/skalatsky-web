How the metrics are computed
Cycle time — median minutes from tasking receipt to disseminated product, across all taskings closed in the month. Baseline (47 min) is the pre-AI watch-floor average.
Tracks fused/day — unique tracks with ≥2 fused sources per 24h.
Analyst hours saved — (baseline minutes per triage − actual) × taskings triaged, per week.
Detection→interdiction conversion — interdictions attributed to a flagged detection ÷ total detections, per month.
Partner contributors — partner nations sharing track data under agreement, counted monthly.
False-positive rate — dark-vessel flags dismissed as false positive ÷ total flags, per month. Falls as the triage loop's dismissals retrain the flag thresholds — the loop improving the model, not just the queue.

All figures are synthetic. Baselines are judgment calls, stated here instead of buried.
How this connects to the other builds
This dashboard is the third panel of one story — and the generic surface underneath it. The dark-vessel triage board produces the detections and the false-positive trend — every dismissal with a reason is training data. The TCPED copilot produces the cycle-time compression; its shadow floor (new routing vs the old greedy rule) is the same comparison this dashboard's baseline-vs-actual chart makes at program scale. Detect → triage → prove it worked.

The live pipeline feed above is the general version of that thread: any component feeds outcome events here via evidence-dashboard.html?events=[...], each shaped as {component, label, outcome: "win"|"loss"|"neutral", detail}. The board aggregates wins and losses per component — so the same surface that scores today's dark-vessel → triage pipeline scores whatever gets built next. One area of improvement is just the first tenant.
